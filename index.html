Ukrainian Cases

Case practice

Free, interactive, and gamified practice pages for English-speaking learners of Ukrainian, working through the grammatical case system one case at a time. Each case comes with its own visual study guide explaining the rules, plus exercises built to drill case usage, word endings, and distinguishing between different cases. Finish an exercise and you'll get a small Ukrainian-themed reward to mark it.

No sign-up, no tracking, no backend; everything runs in your browser and your progress is saved locally on your own device. A family-friendly resource for teachers and learners of Ukrainian language.

Називний відмінок Nominative case

Хто? Що? The dictionary form, and the foundation every other case builds on: gender, plurals, subjects, numerals. Every other page assumes you know this one already, so start here if you're new. Genitive and Accusative are traditionally learned next.
