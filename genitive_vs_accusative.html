Родовий чи Знахідний?

Genitive or Accusative?

Родовий vs Знахідний

This set of exercises exists purely to stop you mixing the two cases up. Every question gives you a sentence with a gap and three word-forms in brackets; tap the one that belongs. Some options are the wrong case entirely (e.g. nominative when you need genitive), some are the right case with the wrong ending, and one is correct. Getting fast and confident at telling Родовий and Знахідний apart, not just forming each one correctly in isolation, is the whole point of this set of exercises.

Sections start with each case reviewed separately, then mix them together, then get hard, including a final section full of nominative «false friends» that look like they need a case change but don't.

📖Швидка шпаргалкаQuick reference

Genitive (Родовий): Кого? Чого?

  • Possession, negation, немає, quantity/measure words, dates (month name), certain prepositions (без, до, від, з, біля, у/в), certain verbs (боятися, досягати, бажати, чекати), numerals 5+ / скільки?

Accusative (Знахідний): Кого? Що? Куди? Коли?

  • Direct object, direction of motion (куди?), days of the week (коли?), sports/games (у що?), verb + preposition (чекати на, сподіватися на, дивитися на, відповідати на).
  • Animacy rule (masculine only): animate = genitive form, inanimate = nominative form.

Watch out for

  • є takes the nominative; only немає takes the genitive.
  • Numerals 2–4 take the nominative plural; only 5+ takes the genitive plural.
  • Comparisons with як keep the nominative.
  • The subject of a sentence is always nominative, animate or not; the animacy rule only ever applies to the object.
